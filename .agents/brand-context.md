## 1. Core Brand Strategy & Positioning

- **Product**: **Speakly** (AI Voice-to-Text Desktop Application with Rubber Ducky Mascot for macOS and Windows).
- **Core Value Proposition**: Speak naturally at 158 WPM. Automatic removal of filler words ("um", "uh") and mid-sentence self-corrections. Instant paste at cursor into any application (Cursor, Slack, Notion, Gmail) without stealing focus.
- **Engine**: Dual-Engine Architecture — Groq LPU (`whisper-large-v3-turbo` + `llama-3.3-70b-versatile`) for ~0.29s latency, with automatic OpenAI Whisper fallback.
- **Brand Personality**: Hyper-fast, technical yet approachable, delightful, zero-slop, developer-friendly.

## 3. IMMUTABLE RULE: The Rubber Ducky Mascot

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **USER EXPLICIT MANDATE:**
> *"Rubber ducky at the bottom of my screen or in the bar every time I press my command button or just that rubber ducky, you know what I mean? That doesn't have to change, by the way. Just so you know. And that needs to stay the same."*

- **Floating Pill HUD Mascot (Bottom Center)**:
  - Must ALWAYS remain the expressive Rubber Ducky mascot.
  - Three animated states:
    1. **Listening**: Rubber ducky wearing blue studio headphones, wide alert eye with catchlights, listening to 16kHz PCM stream.
    2. **Thinking**: Rubber ducky with squinted curious eye and floating cyan/amber thought bubbles during Groq LPU polish.
    3. **Solved / Pasted**: Happy anime eye smile (`^_^`), rosy blush cheeks, golden sparkle of victory above head.
- **Menu Bar / System Tray Icon**:
  - Must ALWAYS remain the cute yellow rubber duck silhouette.
  - Switches to orange (`#FF6B00`) when recording is active.
- **Relationship between Mascot & Brand Kit**:
  - The Mascot is the emotional soul of the product (the friendly Rubber Ducky you talk to).
  - Option 04 provides the brand skin, app icon logo mark, audio frequency bar motif, and cyan/gold cyber-matrix aesthetics.
