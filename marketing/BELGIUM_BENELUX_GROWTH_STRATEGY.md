# Speakly Belgium & Benelux Growth Playbook
*How to turn your home market into your first high-margin customer base with €0 upfront spend.*

---

## 🇧🇪 Why Belgium is the Perfect Early Access Wedge

1. **The Code-Switching Superpower (Dutch + English Tech Jargon)**
   - Almost every developer, marketer, and agency worker in Flanders and Brussels switches fluidly between Dutch and English: *"Kun je die PR even mergen en checken of de backend endpoint goed respondet?"*
   - Apple's built-in dictation fails miserably at this because it forces you to pick either English or Dutch.
   - **Speakly's edge:** Groq Whisper-large-v3 handles multilingual code-switching naturally, and Llama-3.3 cleans up Flemish phrasing into sharp professional Dutch or English prompts.

2. **Compact, High-Trust Tech Communities**
   - Belgium's tech ecosystem is small and tightly connected. A personal recommendation travels fast across Ghent, Antwerp, Brussels, and Leuven.
