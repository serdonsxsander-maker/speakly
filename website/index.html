QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Speakly really 100% free right now? ▼
Yes! During our public Early Access period, Speakly is completely free with unlimited sub-second dictations. You don't need a credit card or your own API keys.
How does Speakly paste without stealing window focus? ▼
The floating pill window is built with Electron's `setFocusable(false)`. When you release your hotkey, Speakly injects the text using native OS keystrokes (`⌘V` / `Ctrl+V`) directly into whichever text field you were already typing in.
What happens to images or links on my clipboard? ▼
Speakly automatically snapshots whatever was on your clipboard before pasting, and restores it in 600ms. Your screenshots, links, and copied code snippets stay completely safe.
Which languages does Speakly support? ▼
Speakly natively supports over 99 languages, including English, Dutch, French, German, Spanish, and Italian. It detects your spoken language automatically.
