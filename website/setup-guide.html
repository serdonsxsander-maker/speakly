🐥 Speakly Prototype Setup Guide

Set Up Speakly on macOS

A quick 2-minute guide for testers to install, activate, and start dictating across any app on their Mac.

🌐

Works on Any Wi-Fi

The app connects directly to cloud speech engines over HTTPS. Use it at home, school, coffee shops, or mobile hotspot—no local network required.

⚡

Zero Re-setup on Reboot

Permissions, hotkeys, and account settings are saved permanently in macOS. Close your laptop lid or restart—it is always ready.

1

Download & Install to Applications

Open the Speakly-1.0.0-arm64.dmg installer disk image you received.

⚠️ Crucial Step: Drag the Speakly duck icon into your Mac's Applications folder. Do NOT run the app directly from inside the DMG disk image. Installing into /Applications allows macOS to permanently remember your permissions across restarts!
  • ✓ Opened the .dmg installer and dragged Speakly into Applications.
2

First Launch (macOS Gatekeeper)

Open your Applications folder and launch Speakly.

First-time macOS prompt: Because Speakly is in Early Access (not yet notarized by Apple), macOS may show a message saying "Apple cannot check it for malicious software" or "App from an unidentified developer".

Option A (Fastest):
  1. Right-click (or Control + click) on Speakly in Applications.
  2. Click Open in the context menu.
  3. Click Open Anyway on the confirmation prompt.
Option B (macOS 15 Sequoia):
Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, and click "Open Anyway" next to Speakly.

Option C (Power users / Terminal):
Run this single command in Terminal:
xattr -cr /Applications/Speakly.app
  • ✓ Launched Speakly and confirmed the one-time open prompt.
3

Connect Your Account (Google or Email)

On first launch, Speakly displays the activation gate.

Click Sign in with Google (or enter your email and password). Your default browser will open to authenticate, then automatically return to Speakly and sync your personalized hotkey and vocabulary.

  • ✓ Logged in with Google or Email (shows green "✓ Account connected").
4

Enable Required macOS Permissions (One-Time)

Speakly requires two standard macOS permissions to listen and paste at your cursor:

1. Microphone Access

Click Allow Microphone and click OK when macOS prompts you.

2. Accessibility & Keystroke Injection

Click Open Accessibility. In macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, ensure Speakly is toggled ON. This allows Speakly to detect when you hold your hotkey and paste text directly where you are typing.

✓ Permanent: Once toggled on, macOS remembers these permissions forever. You will never have to grant them again, even when closing and reopening your laptop!
  • ✓ Microphone access granted.
  • ✓ Accessibility toggled ON in macOS System Settings.
  • ✓ Clicked "✓ Start Using Speakly" to unlock the app.
5

How to Use Speakly Anywhere

Speakly runs quietly in your macOS Menu Bar (look for the yellow duck 🐥 at the top right of your screen).

Push-to-Talk Hotkey
Hold Right ⌥ Option (or Right ⌘ Command)

1. Click your cursor into any text field (Slack, Google Docs, Email, Notes, Terminal, etc.).
2. Press & hold your hotkey. The floating duck indicator appears.
3. Speak naturally in English or any language.
4. Release the key. In ~0.3 seconds, cleaned & grammatically polished text pastes instantly!

Frequently Asked Questions for Testers

Can I use Speakly on other Wi-Fi networks (not just the developer's)?
Yes, absolutely! Speakly communicates directly with cloud-hosted AI speech models (Groq LPU and OpenAI Whisper) and Supabase Auth over secure HTTPS. You can use it on any home Wi-Fi, office network, university campus, coffee shop, or iPhone 5G personal hotspot anywhere in the world.
Do I have to redo this setup when I close my laptop or reboot?
No. Your login session, hotkey preferences, and macOS permissions are saved permanently in your system. Speakly has Launch at Login enabled by default, so whenever your Mac wakes from sleep or boots up, Speakly is already waiting in your top menu bar ready for you to press your hotkey.
How do I change my hotkey or change AI mode?
Click the yellow duck icon 🐥 in your macOS menu bar (top right corner) to bring up the Speakly Dashboard. In Settings, you can switch your hotkey between Right Option, Right Command, or Right Control, or customize your AI dictation mode (Smart Polish, Raw Speech, Email/Slack formatter, etc.).
What if text doesn't paste automatically into my app?
Open macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, remove Speakly and re-add it (or toggle it off and on). macOS occasionally requires a re-toggle if the app binary was updated. Also ensure the text cursor is blinking inside the text field where you want to paste.
